# TripStory — Product Concept

## The job to be done
A parent driving kids a long (or repeated) distance wants them **engaged, calm, and learning** instead of bored, screen-glued, or asking "are we there yet." TripStory hires the road itself as the entertainer.

## What the user experiences
- **Welcome:** the app greets the family warmly and sets up the drive.
- **Narration in place:** as you drive, it tells you where you are and what's around ("on your right, the hills where..."), timed so it lands *as* you pass, not after.
- **Stories:** folk tales, real people who lived here, nature, history, archaeology, the killer must-see points.
- **Engagement loop:** trivia and questions, on new material and on "last time we talked about that."
- **Memory:** it never repeats. On a recurring drive it builds on what the family already learned and calls back to it.
- **Pleasant companionship:** it accompanies the road, it doesn't lecture.

## Content pillars
1. **The road, live** — where you are, left/right, what's coming up.
2. **Stories** — folklore + real notable people of the area.
3. **Nature** — landscape, geology, flora/fauna.
4. **History & archaeology** — what happened here, what's buried here.
5. **Killer points** — the highlights worth flagging.
6. **Trivia & recall** — new questions + memory callbacks.

## Differentiation (the wedge)
- Built **for kids** (tone, length, interaction, safety), where competitors are built for adult travelers.
- **Remembers the family** across trips and never recycles, where competitors are stateless.

## Pricing (to validate)
- 1 free story to hook.
- Then subscription ($x/month) and/or per-story.
- Open question: where the paywall sits so it converts without feeling cheap. Likely: free story must be *the magic one* on a route the family actually drives.

## Age design note
- Target 8-12 first. Audio for 4-7 is a different product (shorter, simpler, more "can you spot"). Grow down later.
- Younger siblings ride along on the 8-12 experience without it being designed for them yet.

## Anti-goals
- Not an adult travel-fact reader (that's Autio's lane).
- Not a navigation app (we sit on top of the map, we don't rebuild it).
- Never narrates an unverified "fact."
