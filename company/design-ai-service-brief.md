## LONG POST — main publishable version (~250 words)

**Most design teams have "started using AI" and gotten nothing real out of it.**

You've got the seats. People paste things into chat, generate a few mockups, maybe summarize a call. Then everyone quietly goes back to working the way they always did. The tools are open in a tab. The work isn't actually faster or better.

Here's the part nobody says out loud: the gap isn't the models. It's the workflow. 88% of orgs use AI. Around 60% report no real value from it. Designers get the worst version of this — stuck choosing between ignoring AI completely, or losing whole afternoons fiddling with prompts and "agents."

So let me be clear about what I don't do. I don't build you an agent army. I don't set up automations you'll have to babysit. You didn't get into design to maintain AI plumbing. You want to do the work.

That's the offer: a working session where I sit with your team and we run AI against your actual backlog. Real research synthesis. Real JTBD. Real problem framing, flows, PRDs, design critique, competitive teardowns. Using a method I've built — the `//` way of working — that skips the prompt-engineering entirely.

Your team leaves having shipped real output that day, and knowing how to repeat it Monday without me.

This is for in-house design leaders who own a team and feel the "use AI" pressure with nothing to show for it.

I'm running a first cohort with limited sessions. If that's you, comment or DM me.
