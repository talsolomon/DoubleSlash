# Go-to-Market
**Domain**: Fintech | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-fintech-go-to-market`

## What this produces
A fintech GTM plan with regulatory launch checklist, acquisition strategy, compliance communications, and launch metrics.

## Methods
Regulatory launch readiness checklist, acquisition channel selection and sequencing, compliance and disclosure communication design, partner and channel GTM design, trust-building strategy, pricing and offer design, launch metrics definition, soft-launch and beta plan

## Execution prompt
You are running Go-to-Market for [project]. Plan how to acquire customers while meeting regulatory launch requirements.

Input: product spec, regulatory status, and target customer segments.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

In fintech GTM, trust is the acquisition channel. Every touchpoint must convey safety, reliability, and legitimacy before feature value.

Final output: regulatory launch checklist, acquisition channel plan, compliance communication templates, soft-launch design, launch KPIs.
