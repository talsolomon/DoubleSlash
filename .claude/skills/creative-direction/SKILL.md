# Creative Direction Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — map creative territory
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-research` — Gather inspiration, references, and competitive creative benchmarks
- `/ds-creative-direction-inspiration-mapping` — Map visual and conceptual territory to define the creative space

## Define — brief the creative work
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-brief` — Write the brief that directs creative work: objective, audience, tone, constraints
- `/ds-creative-direction-concept-development` — Develop and evaluate creative concepts against the brief

## Develop — direct execution
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-execution` — Direct the production of creative assets with quality gates
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-review` — Evaluate creative work against the brief and provide actionable feedback

## Deliver — produce and learn
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-production` — Manage final production, delivery, and asset archiving
- `/ds-creative-direction-creative-retrospective` — Grade creative outcomes and process quality; capture improvements

**How to run:** type the skill name (e.g. `/ds-creative-direction`) or invoke via `/ds-creative-direction`
