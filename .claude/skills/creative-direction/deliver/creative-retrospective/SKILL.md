# Creative Retrospective
**Domain**: Creative Direction | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-creative-direction-creative-retrospective`

## What this produces
A creative retrospective with performance outcomes, creative decision review, concept-to-execution gap analysis, process assessment, team feedback synthesis, and a brief for the next cycle.

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Depth |
|---|---|
| Nemo | What worked / what didn't, top 3 creative lessons |
| Tuna | Performance review, concept review, process assessment |
| Salmon | Full retrospective with brief quality assessment, team feedback synthesis |
| Willy | All methods — full creative effectiveness analysis with next-cycle brief |

---

## Execution Prompt

You are running a Creative Retrospective for [project]. Close the loop on creative decisions and performance outcomes so the next campaign starts smarter.

**Input**: campaign performance data, all creative assets produced, original brief, production timeline actuals, team feedback (if available).
