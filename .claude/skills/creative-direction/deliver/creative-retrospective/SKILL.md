# Creative Retrospective
**Domain**: Creative Direction | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-creative-direction-creative-retrospective`

## What this produces
A creative retrospective with performance outcomes, creative decision review, process assessment, and lessons for the next campaign.

## Methods
Creative performance data review, concept-to-execution gap analysis, brand consistency review, audience response analysis, production process review, brief quality assessment, creative team feedback synthesis

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | What worked / what didn't, top 3 creative lessons |
| Tuna | Performance review, concept review, process assessment |
| Salmon | Full retrospective with brief quality, team feedback |
| Willy | All methods — full creative effectiveness analysis |

## Execution prompt
You are running a Creative Retrospective for [project]. Close the loop on creative decisions and outcomes.

Input: campaign performance data, creative assets produced, and original brief.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Final output: creative performance summary, 3 decisions that worked (keep), 3 that didn't (change), top process improvement, brief for the next cycle.
