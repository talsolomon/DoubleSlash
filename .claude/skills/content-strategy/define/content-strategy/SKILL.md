# Content Strategy
**Domain**: Content Strategy | **Phase**: Define | **Invocation**: `/ds-content-strategy-content-strategy`

## What this produces
A content strategy document with goals, audience definition, content pillars, format mix, distribution channels, and success metrics.

## Methods
Content goal setting, content pillar design, audience definition, format strategy, distribution channel selection, SEO strategy, thought leadership positioning, content funnel mapping, competitive content analysis, measurement framework

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Content goals, 3 pillars, top 2 formats, primary channel |
| Tuna | Full strategy: goals, pillars, formats, channels, metrics |
| Salmon | Strategy with SEO approach, content funnel, thought leadership position |
| Willy | All methods — competitive analysis, full measurement framework |

## Execution prompt
You are running Content Strategy for [project]. Define a content program that serves audience needs and business goals.

Input: audience research, business goals, and available resources.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Final output: content mission statement, 3–5 content pillars, format strategy, primary distribution channels, 3 success metrics with targets.
