# Content Strategy Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — understand content and audience
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-audit` — Inventory and grade existing content for quality, gaps, and ROI
- `/ds-content-strategy-audience-research` — Understand who the content is for, what they need, and how they consume

## Define — set strategy and standards
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-strategy` — Set content purpose, pillars, formats, and distribution model
- `/ds-content-strategy-editorial-guidelines` — Write the rules for voice, tone, quality, and editorial standards

## Develop — produce content
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-planning` — Build the content calendar with themes, formats, and ownership
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-creation` — Produce content to brief, format spec, and editorial guidelines

## Deliver — distribute and measure
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-distribution` — Execute the distribution plan across channels and syndication
- `/ds-content-strategy-content-performance` — Measure content impact and produce optimization recommendations
