# PR Campaign Design
**Domain**: PR/Communications | **Phase**: Develop | **Invocation**: `/ds-pr-communications-pr-campaign-design`

## What this produces
A PR campaign design with news hook, asset plan, media targets, timeline, and earned/owned/paid media strategy.

## Methods
News hook development, campaign narrative design, asset planning (press release, media kit, visuals), influencer and media target identification, embargo and exclusive strategy, owned channel integration, paid amplification design, campaign timeline design

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | News hook, asset list, media targets, timeline |
| Tuna | Campaign design with embargo strategy, owned channel plan |
| Salmon | Full design with influencer strategy, paid amplification |
| Willy | All methods — full asset plan, exclusive strategy, multi-market adaptation |

## Execution prompt
You are running PR Campaign Design for [project]. Design the campaign that earns attention and drives the narrative.

Input: message architecture, media landscape, and announcement details.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

The hook is everything. A technically correct press release with no hook gets no coverage. Lead with what makes this genuinely newsworthy.

Final output: news hook (one sentence), campaign narrative, asset list, media target tier-1/2/3 breakdown, timeline, owned channel integration.
