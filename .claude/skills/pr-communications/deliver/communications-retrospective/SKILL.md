# Communications Retrospective
**Domain**: PR/Communications | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-pr-communications-communications-retrospective`

## What this produces
A communications retrospective with coverage performance, message pull-through analysis, relationship health assessment, and strategy refinements.

## Methods
Coverage volume and quality review, message pull-through analysis, sentiment trend review, relationship health assessment, share of voice analysis, campaign ROI review, crisis and issue management review, strategy refinement recommendations

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Coverage summary, message pull-through, top 3 adjustments |
| Tuna | Retro with sentiment trends, relationship health |
| Salmon | Full retro with share of voice, campaign ROI |
| Willy | All methods — crisis review, full strategy refinement, next-year plan |

## Execution prompt
You are running Communications Retrospective for [project]. Assess whether communications moved the needle and what to do differently.

Input: coverage data, campaign results, and reputation tracking.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Final output: coverage performance summary, message pull-through score, sentiment trends, share of voice vs. competitors, top 3 strategy adjustments for next period.
