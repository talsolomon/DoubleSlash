### Government Grant Considerations

**Pros:**
- Largest individual grant sizes available
- Can fund at scale and systems level
- Legitimizes the organization with other funders

**Cons:**
- Compliance burden (reporting, audits, procurement rules) requires staff capacity
- Often restricted to specific activities and geographies
- Payment in arrears — requires working capital
- Relationship with implementing country government often required
- Long cycle: proposal to award typically 6–18 months

**Readiness assessment for government grants:**

| Requirement | Current status | Gap |

## Phase 4 — Major Donor Segment Analysis

### Major Donor Definition

Major donors: individuals giving ≥ $10,000/year (adjust threshold to organizational context)

### Wealth Screening Framework

Indicators of major donor capacity (accessible via DonorSearch, iWave, or WealthEngine):

| Indicator | What it signals | Source |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Real estate holdings | Wealth proxy — value and number of properties | Public records |
| SEC filings / stock ownership | Liquid wealth in publicly-traded companies | SEC EDGAR |
| Political giving | Philanthropic behavior; FEC records show giving amounts | FEC.gov |
| Prior charitable giving | Philanthropic track record | 990 database / DonorSearch |
| Board memberships (other nonprofits) | Cause interest; peer network | 990 filings |
| Business ownership / executive role | Income proxy | LinkedIn / Crunchbase |

### Major Donor Segment Profiles

| Segment | Profile | Motivations | Cultivation approach | Ask range |
|---------|---------|------------|---------------------|----------|
| Founder / entrepreneur | Sold business; first-time major philanthropy | Legacy, impact, learning | Education-first; site visits; advisory role | $25K–$500K |
| Inherited wealth | Foundation or DAF; long philanthropy history | Family tradition; board engagement | Peer introduction; gala cultivation | $10K–$100K |
| Tech / finance executive | High income; younger; data-driven | Effectiveness, scale, metrics | EA community; GiveWell alignment; outcome data | $10K–$250K |
| Locally connected | Cares about geography; community pride | Local impact; recognition | Community events; naming opportunities | $10K–$100K |
