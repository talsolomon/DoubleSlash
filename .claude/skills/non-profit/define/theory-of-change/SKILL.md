# Theory of Change
**Domain**: Non-Profit | **Phase**: Define | **Invocation**: `/ds-non-profit-theory-of-change`

## What this produces
A theory of change document with inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact — plus key assumptions and risk factors.

## Methods
Logic model design, causal pathway mapping, assumption identification and testing, outcome hierarchy development, counterfactual analysis, risk and vulnerability assessment, indicator development, stakeholder validation approach

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Logic model, causal pathway, top 5 assumptions |
| Tuna | Theory of change with outcome hierarchy, risk factors |
| Salmon | Full ToC with counterfactual, assumption testing |
| Willy | All methods — full logic model, indicator set, validation process |

## Execution prompt
You are running Theory of Change for [project]. Articulate the pathway from what we do to the impact we want.

Input: impact research, program design, and organizational capacity.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

A theory of change is only useful if it's falsifiable. For each step in the chain, ask: what would we see if this assumption were wrong?

Final output: theory of change map (inputs→activities→outputs→outcomes→impact), key assumptions, risk factors, indicator set, validation approach.
