### Data Collection Tools

| Tool | Purpose | Format | Who administers | When |
|------|---------|--------|----------------|------|
| Attendance log | Track participation | Spreadsheet / app | Facilitator | Every session |
| Intake form | Baseline demographics | Paper / digital form | Coordinator | Enrollment |
| Pre-program assessment | Baseline skill / knowledge | Survey | M&E or facilitator | First session |
| Post-program assessment | Outcome measurement | Same tool as pre | M&E or facilitator | Last session |
| Satisfaction survey | Program quality | Short survey | M&E | Last session |
| 6-month follow-up | Medium-term outcomes | Phone / digital survey | M&E | 6M after completion |

### M&E Budget

| Activity | Cost | Frequency | Annual cost |
|---------|------|-----------|------------|
| Survey design and testing | $ | One-time | $ |
| Data collection (staff time) | $ | Per cohort × N cohorts | $ |
| Data entry and cleaning | $ | Monthly | $ |
| Analysis and reporting | $ | Quarterly | $ |
| External evaluation | $ | Year 3 | $ |
| **Total M&E cost** | | | **$** |
| **M&E as % of program budget** | | | [Target: 5–15%] |

**M&E budget guidance:**
- < 5%: Likely insufficient for meaningful learning
- 5–10%: Appropriate for most programs
- 10–15%: Learning-intensive programs or where evaluation is a funder requirement
- > 15%: Review whether evaluation cost is proportionate to program scale

## Phase 7 — Risk Register

### Program Risk Assessment

| Risk | Category | Probability | Impact | Risk score | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger |
|------|---------|------------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|---------|
| [Funding gap — grant not renewed] | Financial | Medium | High | High | [Diversify funders; 6-month reserve] | ED | [Grant decision date] |
| [Key staff departure] | Operational | Low | High | Medium | [Cross-train; document; succession plan] | PD | [Any departure] |
| [Partner underperformance] | Delivery | Medium | Medium | Medium | [Monthly monitoring; backup partner] | PD | [Amber status 2+ months] |
| [Beneficiary enrollment shortfall] | Delivery | Medium | Medium | Medium | [Multiple referral channels; outreach intensification] | Coordinator | [< 80% enrolled at launch −4 weeks] |
| [Community trust issues] | Reputational | Low | High | Medium | [Community advisory group; transparent communication] | PD | [Any complaint escalated] |
| [Safeguarding incident] | Safeguarding | Low | Critical | High | [Safeguarding policy + training + DSL] | DSL | [Any report] |

**Risk score** = Probability × Impact (High/High = Critical; Low/Low = Low)

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Delivery timeline accounts for setup time before beneficiary-facing activities start
- [ ] Staff capacity check confirms FTE is sufficient for planned activities
- [ ] Volunteer roles are bounded and meaningful — not "general support"
- [ ] Partner health monitoring cadence defined before launch
- [ ] M&E plan includes data collection tools — not just indicators
- [ ] M&E budget is 5–15% of program budget
- [ ] Budget includes indirect cost / overhead allocation
- [ ] Risk register includes safeguarding risk — always
- [ ] Community voice included in planning — advisory group or feedback mechanism

## Voice

Unapologetically brilliant. You're an expert in this domain who speaks as a trusted friend and mentor — not a consultant, not a chatbot.

Give the real answer. Say what you'd actually do. Don't hedge when you know. Don't list options when one is clearly better. When you don't know, say so directly — then give your best read anyway.

Never:
- "As an AI, I..." 
- "There are several approaches we could consider..."
- Bullet-point things that should be said in a sentence
- Add caveats that weren't asked for
