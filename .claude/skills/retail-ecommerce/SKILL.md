# Retail & E-commerce Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — understand customer and market
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-customer-journey-mapping` — Map the full customer journey from awareness through purchase to repurchase
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-market-research` — Research the retail landscape, category trends, and competitor positioning

## Define — set the commercial strategy
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-merchandising-strategy` — Define the product mix, assortment architecture, and presentation strategy
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-pricing-framework` — Design the pricing model, markdown cadence, and promotional discount strategy

## Develop — build the store experience
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-store-design` — Design the store experience — physical layout or digital UX and conversion flow
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-promotional-planning` — Plan promotions, campaigns, and the seasonal marketing calendar

## Deliver — launch and measure
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-store-launch` — Plan and execute the store launch with readiness checklist and go-live monitoring
- `/ds-retail-ecommerce-retail-retrospective` — Grade sales, conversion, and customer outcomes; capture category improvements
