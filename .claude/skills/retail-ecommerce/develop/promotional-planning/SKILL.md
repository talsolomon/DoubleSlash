# Promotional Planning
**Domain**: Retail/Ecommerce | **Phase**: Develop | **Invocation**: `/ds-retail-ecommerce-promotional-planning`

## What this produces
A promotional plan with calendar, campaign types, discount strategy, channel execution, and margin impact model.

## Methods
Promotional calendar design, campaign type taxonomy, discount depth and frequency strategy, margin impact modeling, email and SMS campaign design, paid media amplification, loyalty program integration, urgency and scarcity design

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Promo calendar, campaign types, discount rules |
| Tuna | Promotional plan with email design, margin model |
| Salmon | Full plan with paid amplification, loyalty integration |
| Willy | All methods — full channel execution, urgency design, margin protection |

## Execution prompt
You are running Promotional Planning for [project]. Design promotions that drive revenue without training customers to wait for sales.

Input: pricing framework, merchandising strategy, and seasonality data.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Promotion frequency trains buyer behavior. Plan promotions that create events, not expectations.

Final output: 12-month promotional calendar, campaign types with discount rules, margin impact model, channel execution plan, loyalty integration.
