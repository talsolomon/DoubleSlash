# Audience Research
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Discover | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-audience-research`

## What this produces
An audience research brief with segment profiles, consumption behavior, needs and motivations, and editorial opportunity map.

## Methods
Audience segmentation, behavioral data analysis, survey and interview synthesis, platform analytics review, competitor audience comparison, need-state mapping, topic affinity analysis, engagement pattern analysis

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Audience segments, top consumption behaviors, editorial opportunities |
| Tuna | Research brief with need-states, platform analytics |
| Salmon | Full research with topic affinity, competitor comparison |
| Willy | All methods — full segment profiles, behavioral deep-dive, opportunity map |

## Execution prompt
You are running Audience Research for [project]. Understand who the audience is and what content they want.

Input: media property context, available analytics, and research access.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Audiences don't always say what they want. Behavioral data beats stated preferences — use both.

Final output: audience segment profiles, top consumption behaviors, need-state map, editorial opportunity analysis, content gap identification.
