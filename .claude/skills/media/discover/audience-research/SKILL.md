# Audience Research
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Discover | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-audience-research`

## What this produces
An audience research brief with segment profiles, consumption behavior maps, need-state taxonomy, platform analytics review, competitor audience comparison, and editorial opportunity map.

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Depth |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Audience segments, top consumption behaviors, editorial opportunities |
| Tuna | Research brief with need-states, platform analytics, behavioral data |
| Salmon | Full research with topic affinity, competitor audience comparison |
| Willy | All methods — full segment profiles, behavioral deep-dive, opportunity map |

---

## Execution Prompt

You are running Audience Research for [media property/publication]. Understand who the audience is, how they consume content, and what jobs they need content to do. Behavioral data beats stated preferences — use both.

**Input**: media property context, available analytics data, research access (interviews, surveys, community observation).

---

### 1. Audience Segmentation

Define 2-4 distinct audience segments. Each segment should have meaningfully different content needs.

**Segment profile template** (repeat per segment):

**Segment [A/B/C]: "[Name]"**
- **Who they are**: [Demographics: age range, role/profession, life situation]
- **What brings them here**: [The trigger that leads them to this media property]
- **Content need**: [What job does content do for them — inform / entertain / validate / connect / learn]
- **Consumption behavior**: [When, where, how much — e.g., "reads Monday morning, 10-min sessions, mobile"]
- **Format preference**: [Long-form / Short / Video / Audio / Visual]
- **Sophistication level**: [Beginner / Intermediate / Expert on the topic]
- **Size estimate**: [% of total audience or absolute number]

---

### 2. Need-State Taxonomy

Map the emotional and functional jobs the audience needs content to perform.

| Need-state | Description | Content that serves it | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Orientation** | "What's happening in [topic] right now?" | News, digests, weekly roundups | High |
| **Depth** | "I want to understand this properly" | Long-form analysis, explainers | High |
| **Validation** | "Am I seeing this right?" | Opinion, commentary, expert takes | Medium |
| **Application** | "How do I do this?" | Tutorials, how-tos, playbooks | Medium |
| **Connection** | "I want to be part of this community" | Discussion, community content | Low-Med |
| **Discovery** | "Show me something I didn't know I needed" | Curation, roundups | Medium |

**Underserved need-states** (where competitors aren't meeting the audience):
1. [Need-state]: [Gap in current content landscape]
