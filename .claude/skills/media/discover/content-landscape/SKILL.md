# Content Landscape
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Discover | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-content-landscape`

## What this produces
A content landscape map with competitor analysis, format and platform coverage, content gaps, and differentiation opportunities.

## Methods
Competitor content audit, platform coverage mapping, format and frequency analysis, topic and angle mapping, audience overlap analysis, content quality benchmarking, monetization model survey, white space identification

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Top competitors, format landscape, content white space |
| Tuna | Landscape with audience overlap, quality benchmarks |
| Salmon | Full map with platform coverage, monetization models |
| Willy | All methods — full competitor audit, topic map, differentiation strategy |

## Execution prompt
You are running Content Landscape for [project]. Map what content exists and where the opportunity to stand out lies.

Input: media category and target audience.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

A content landscape analysis that doesn't identify differentiation isn't useful — the goal is to find where you can win.

Final output: competitor map, format and platform coverage, content gap analysis, top 3 differentiation opportunities.
