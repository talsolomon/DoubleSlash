# Content Landscape
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Discover | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-content-landscape`

## What this produces
A content landscape map with competitor profiles, format/platform coverage matrix, content gap analysis, quality benchmarks, monetization model survey, and top 3 differentiation opportunities.

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Depth |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Top competitors, format landscape, content white space |
| Tuna | Landscape with audience overlap, quality benchmarks, platform coverage |
| Salmon | Full map with monetization models, topic map, differentiation strategy |
| Willy | All methods — full competitor audit, editorial calendar opportunities |

---

## Execution Prompt

You are running Content Landscape for [media category/topic area]. Map what content exists and where the opportunity to stand out lies. A landscape analysis that doesn't identify differentiation isn't useful — the goal is to find where you can win.

**Input**: media category and target audience.
