# Distribution Planning
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Develop | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-distribution-planning`

## What this produces
A distribution plan with channel strategy, platform-specific adaptation approach, syndication opportunities, and reach projections.

## Methods
Channel selection and prioritization, platform algorithm analysis, SEO and discoverability strategy, social distribution design, newsletter and owned channel strategy, syndication partner identification, paid amplification planning, cross-promotion strategy

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Top channels, platform approach, reach targets |
| Tuna | Distribution plan with SEO strategy, newsletter design |
| Salmon | Full plan with syndication partners, paid amplification |
| Willy | All methods — full channel model, algorithm optimization, cross-promotion |

## Execution prompt
You are running Distribution Planning for [project]. Get the right content to the right audience on the right platforms.

Input: editorial strategy, audience research, and available channels.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Distribution is not an afterthought. The best content with no distribution strategy reaches no one.

Final output: channel strategy, platform-specific approach, SEO priorities, newsletter plan, syndication opportunities, reach projections.
