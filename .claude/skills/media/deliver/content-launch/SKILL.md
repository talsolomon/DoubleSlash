# Content Launch
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-content-launch`

## What this produces
A content launch plan with pre-launch buildup, day-one content package, promotional strategy, and early traction metrics.

## Methods
Pre-launch buildup strategy, launch content package design, partner and media outreach, social and community launch activation, press and PR strategy, early-access and beta audience design, launch event planning, first-week monitoring plan

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Launch content package, promotion plan, traction metrics |
| Tuna | Launch plan with partner outreach, community activation |
| Salmon | Full plan with press strategy, early-access design |
| Willy | All methods — full launch campaign, event plan, partner network activation |

## Execution prompt
You are running Content Launch for [project]. Launch the property or series in a way that builds lasting audience.

Input: editorial strategy, distribution plan, and promotional assets.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

The first week defines audience perception. Launch with enough content that new visitors can immediately understand what this is about and why to return.

Final output: pre-launch checklist, launch content package, promotional plan, partner outreach list, first-week monitoring dashboard.
