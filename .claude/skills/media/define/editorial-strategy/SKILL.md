# Editorial Strategy
**Domain**: Media | **Phase**: Define | **Invocation**: `/ds-media-editorial-strategy`

## What this produces
An editorial strategy document with mission, audience promise, content pillars, editorial voice, and coverage principles.

## Methods
Editorial mission definition, audience promise articulation, content pillar design, editorial voice and tone definition, topic scope setting, editorial calendar structure, coverage principles and exclusions, competitive differentiation statement

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Mission, 3 content pillars, voice definition |
| Tuna | Editorial strategy with audience promise, coverage scope |
| Salmon | Full strategy with calendar structure, differentiation |
| Willy | All methods — editorial handbook, voice guide, full coverage principles |

## Execution prompt
You are running Editorial Strategy for [project]. Define what this media property stands for and what it covers.

Input: audience research, content landscape, and organizational mission.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

A clear editorial strategy makes every content decision faster. If you can't say what you're NOT covering, the strategy isn't sharp enough.

Final output: editorial mission, audience promise, 3–5 content pillars, voice and tone guide, coverage principles and exclusions.
