# Policy Writing
**Domain**: Legal/Compliance | **Phase**: Define | **Invocation**: `/ds-legal-compliance-policy-writing`

## What this produces
Policy documents with purpose, scope, obligations, enforcement mechanism, and version control structure.

## Methods
Policy scope and purpose definition, obligation articulation, exception and escalation design, enforcement mechanism writing, review and approval workflow, version control setup, plain-language rewrite, legal review checklist

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Policy purpose, scope, top obligations |
| Tuna | Full policy document with enforcement, exceptions |
| Salmon | Policy with version control, review workflow, plain-language |
| Willy | All methods — full policy suite, legal review checklist, training companion |

## Execution prompt
You are running Policy Writing for [project]. Draft the policy documents that define how things must work.

Input: regulatory requirements, organizational context, and policy type.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Write for the person who will be bound by it, not the lawyer who reviewed it. Clarity reduces violations.

Final output: policy document (purpose, scope, obligations, exceptions, enforcement), review checklist, version history template.
