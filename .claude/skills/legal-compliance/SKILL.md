# Legal & Compliance Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

- `/ds-legal-compliance-regulatory-research` — Research applicable laws, regulations, and legal precedents
- `/ds-legal-compliance-risk-assessment` — Identify and assess legal and compliance risks with mitigation options

## Define — build the compliance framework
- `/ds-legal-compliance-compliance-framework` — Design the compliance program, controls, and accountability structure

## Deliver — audit and improve
- `/ds-legal-compliance-compliance-audit` — Audit compliance posture against requirements and internal controls
- `/ds-legal-compliance-legal-retrospective` — Grade legal outcomes and identify process and coverage improvements
