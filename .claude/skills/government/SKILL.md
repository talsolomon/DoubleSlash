# Government & Policy Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — research policy and stakeholders
- `/ds-government-policy-research` — Research policy landscape, precedents, international comparisons, and evidence base
- `/ds-government-stakeholder-mapping` — Map all stakeholders, their interests, influence levels, and positions

## Define — design the policy
- `/ds-government-policy-framework` — Design the policy framework, principles, and implementation model
- `/ds-government-regulatory-design` — Design regulations with enforceability, compliance cost, and outcomes in mind

## Develop — plan consultation and rollout
- `/ds-government-policy-planning` — Plan the policy development process, workstreams, and timeline
- `/ds-government-public-consultation` — Design and run the public consultation with analysis and response framework

## Deliver — implement and review
- `/ds-government-policy-implementation` — Plan and manage policy rollout with accountability and monitoring
- `/ds-government-policy-review` — Evaluate policy outcomes against objectives and recommend adjustments
