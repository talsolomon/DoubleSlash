# Public Consultation
**Domain**: Government | **Phase**: Develop | **Invocation**: `/ds-government-public-consultation`

## What this produces
A public consultation plan with process design, questions, engagement channels, synthesis methodology, and response framework.

## Methods
Consultation obligation review, target audience segmentation, channel selection (online/in-person/written), question design, accessibility planning, response analysis methodology, feedback synthesis framework, policy update decision process

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Consultation questions, channels, response plan |
| Tuna | Consultation plan with audience segmentation, synthesis method |
| Salmon | Full plan with accessibility, feedback decision process |
| Willy | All methods — full channel strategy, public report design, policy update process |

## Execution prompt
You are running Public Consultation for [project]. Design a consultation that genuinely informs the policy, not just checks a box.

Input: policy design, stakeholder map, and legal consultation obligations.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Consultations that ignore feedback destroy trust. Design with a clear decision about how responses will change the policy.

Final output: consultation design, question set, channel plan, synthesis methodology, response commitment framework.
