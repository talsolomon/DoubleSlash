# Operations Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — assess operations
- `/ds-operations-process-audit` — Audit processes for efficiency, quality, bottlenecks, and compliance
- `/ds-operations-operations-research` — Research operational benchmarks, best practices, and gap analysis

## Define — design the process
- `/ds-operations-process-design` — Design the target process with roles, handoffs, and decision points
- `/ds-operations-sop-writing` — Write standard operating procedures that are clear and executable

## Develop — plan execution
- `/ds-operations-operations-planning` — Plan the operational model, team structure, and resourcing
- `/ds-operations-resource-planning` — Plan resource allocation, capacity, and hiring against targets

## Deliver — launch and improve
- `/ds-operations-operations-launch` — Plan and execute the operational launch with readiness checklist
- `/ds-operations-operations-retrospective` — Grade operational outcomes and identify process improvements
