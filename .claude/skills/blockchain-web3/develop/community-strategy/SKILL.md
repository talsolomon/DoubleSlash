# Community Strategy
**Domain**: Blockchain/Web3 | **Phase**: Develop | **Invocation**: `/ds-blockchain-web3-community-strategy`

## What this produces
A community strategy with growth channels, engagement mechanics, contributor incentives, governance participation design, and community KPIs.

## Methods
Community platform strategy (Discord/Twitter/Farcaster/Mirror), ambassador program design, contributor incentive design, content and education strategy, governance participation incentives, community event design, influencer and KOL strategy, community health metrics

## FISH Guide
| Fish | Run these methods |
|---|---|
| Nemo | Platform strategy, top 3 engagement mechanics, growth channels |
| Tuna | Strategy with ambassador program, contributor incentives |
| Salmon | Full strategy with governance participation, KOL approach |
| Willy | All methods — full contributor program, community health metrics, event calendar |

## Execution prompt
You are running Community Strategy for [project]. Build the community that drives protocol adoption and governance participation.

Input: protocol design and target community segments.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Web3 communities are also user bases, governance bodies, and distribution channels simultaneously. Design for all three roles.

Final output: platform strategy, growth channels, engagement mechanics, contributor incentive design, governance participation strategy, community KPIs.
