# Token Launch
**Domain**: Blockchain/Web3 | **Phase**: Deliver | **Invocation**: `/ds-blockchain-web3-token-launch`

## What this produces
A token launch plan with TGE design, distribution strategy, liquidity seeding approach, listing strategy, and post-launch monitoring.

## Methods
TGE structure design, distribution event mechanics (IDO/IEO/LBP/airdrop), initial liquidity pool design, CEX and DEX listing strategy, vesting schedule implementation, anti-dump mechanism design, post-launch price stability approach, community and press communications

| Fish | Run these methods |

## Execution prompt
You are running Token Launch for [project]. Design a token launch that establishes fair distribution and lasting liquidity.

Input: protocol design, tokenomics model, and community strategy.
FISH classification: [Nemo/Tuna/Salmon/Willy]

Token launches fail most often from sybil attacks, concentrated dumps, or liquidity fragility. Address all three explicitly.

Final output: TGE structure, distribution mechanics, initial liquidity design, listing strategy, vesting enforcement, anti-dump provisions, post-launch monitoring plan.
