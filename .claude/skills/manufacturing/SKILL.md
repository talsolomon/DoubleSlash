# Manufacturing Skills

**8 skills across 4 phases.** Pick the one that matches where you are.

## Discover — assess production and supply chain
- `/ds-manufacturing-production-audit` — Assess current production capacity, quality metrics, and efficiency gaps
- `/ds-manufacturing-supply-chain-research` — Map the supply chain, identify risks, and evaluate alternatives

## Define — specify quality and production standards
- `/ds-manufacturing-manufacturing-spec` — Write the product manufacturing specification with tolerances and standards
- `/ds-manufacturing-quality-framework` — Design the quality management system, standards, and inspection protocols

## Develop — plan production
- `/ds-manufacturing-production-planning` — Plan production runs, capacity allocation, and scheduling
- `/ds-manufacturing-supplier-evaluation` — Evaluate and select suppliers against quality, cost, and risk criteria

## Deliver — launch and improve quality
- `/ds-manufacturing-production-launch` — Plan and execute the production launch with readiness gates
- `/ds-manufacturing-quality-retrospective` — Grade quality outcomes, root-cause defects, and capture improvements
